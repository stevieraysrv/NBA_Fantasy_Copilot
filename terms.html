Updated: 21 October 2026. Fantasy NBA Copilot is an independent prototype for basketball projection comparisons. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Yahoo or the NBA.

Demonstration data

The bundled league, players, and statistics are fictional. The demo is not connected to Yahoo Fantasy. Imported and edited data is your responsibility; use information you are entitled to process and keep private league data out of public repositories.

Local simulations

Trades, player moves, and waiver comparisons are calculations in your browser. They do not submit transactions or modify a Yahoo league. Review actual player availability, league rules, and projections in Yahoo before making decisions.

Accuracy and availability

Outputs depend on your inputs and the model's assumptions. They are not guaranteed outcomes. The model does not account for all lineup, schedule, injury, eligibility, or scoring details. Local browser storage can be cleared or lost; export a backup if you want to keep changes.

API access

Access to Yahoo's API is subject to Yahoo's approval and terms. This demo does not grant API access. Any future account integration requires additional backend work and updated data-handling documentation.
